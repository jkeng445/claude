# Selling this as a service

If you run this kit for clients, here's how to package it so it pays and stays deliverable.

> **Prices below are planning ranges, not market research.** Rates vary a lot by country, niche, and track record. Test them with your first 3 clients and adjust. If you're new, price toward the low end in exchange for a testimonial and permission to share results.

---

## Three packages

| Package | What the client gets | Your time (est.) | Starting price range |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Presence Setup** (one-off, 2 weeks) | Intake call, positioning worksheet, handles claimed and secured, profiles fully set up on 1–2 platforms, bio + link hub, 9 launch posts, 30-day calendar | 10–15 hrs | $400–1,500 |
| **2. Launch & Coach** (setup + 90 days) | Package 1, plus fortnightly scorecard reviews, feedback on their posts, and a monthly strategy call. **The client posts themselves.** | Setup + ~3 hrs/month | $1,200–3,500 total |
| **3. Done-for-you** (monthly retainer, 3-month minimum) | Package 1, plus you write and schedule 12–16 posts/month, monthly report. CEOs: ghostwriting from a weekly 30-min interview. | 10–20 hrs/month | $800–3,000+/month |

**Who fits which:**
- **Writers** usually buy Package 1 or 2. They have smaller budgets and the voice must be theirs.
- **Brands** usually buy Package 3 once they have sales. Package 1 before that.
- **CEOs** buy Package 3 (ghostwriting). They have the least time and the highest value per opportunity.

## What you never promise
- A follower number. Promise **deliverables and a process**, and report the result metric honestly.
- "Going viral."
- Results from platforms changing their rules overnight. Put a sentence in the contract.

## Contract must-haves (one page is fine)
1. Scope: platforms, number of posts, number of revisions, response times.
2. **The client owns their accounts, content, and passwords.** You get delegated access, which is revoked at the end.
3. Approval: nothing posts without client approval (essential for CEOs).
4. Payment: 50% upfront for setup, retainers paid monthly in advance.
5. Notice period: 30 days.
6. Confidentiality, especially for CEO clients.

## Your own presence (do this first)
You're selling presence, so yours *is* the portfolio.
- Run the **CEO playbook on yourself** on LinkedIn, plus Instagram if your clients are creators and brands.
- Document each client's 90 days as a case study (with permission): before/after profile screenshots, the scorecard, a client quote.
- Three case studies make selling much easier.

## Client pipeline: first 90 days
1. Weeks 1–2: set up your own profiles with this kit; post 3×/week.
2. Weeks 2–4: offer Package 1 at a **founding rate** to 5 people in your network (one writer, one brand, one CEO if possible) in exchange for a testimonial.
3. Weeks 4–12: post their results (anonymised if needed), and ask every happy client for one referral.
4. Raise prices once you're booked out for 3 weeks in a row.

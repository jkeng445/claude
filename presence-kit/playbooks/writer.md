# Playbook: Writer

Authors, novelists, journalists, poets, essayists, ghostwriters.

**The result that matters: email subscribers.** Publishers, agents, and editors increasingly ask "what's your platform?" Their real question is "how many people will buy when you publish?" An email list answers that. Social media feeds the list.

---

## 1. Platform choice

| Writer type | Main | Second | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fiction / romance / fantasy / YA** | Instagram + TikTok (BookTok) | Substack | Readers find fiction through mood, tropes, and aesthetics |
| **Literary fiction / poetry** | Substack | Instagram or Threads | The work itself is the content; short excerpts travel |
| **Non-fiction / business / self-help** | LinkedIn or X | Substack | Ideas travel as threads and posts; expertise builds authority |
| **Journalist / essayist** | X or Bluesky + Substack | LinkedIn | Editors and sources live there |

**Always:** Substack (or Beehiiv / Kit) as home base, plus one social platform. Add a second only after 8 consistent weeks.

## 2. Positioning formula

> I write **[genre / topic]** for **[specific reader]** who **[what they crave or care about]**.

Examples:
- "I write slow-burn fantasy romance for readers who want the yearning *and* the politics."
- "I write about money for first-generation professionals who never got the talk at home."

## 3. Profile setup

**Name:** your pen name, exactly as it will appear on the book cover.
**Handle:** `@firstnamelastname`, or add `writes`, `author`, or `books` if taken (`@janedoewrites`).
**Photo:** head and shoulders, natural light, plain background, looking at the camera.

**Bio formulas (pick one):**
- `[Genre] author · [hook or trope] · [credential or work-in-progress] ↓ [free thing]`
  > *Fantasy romance author · enemies-to-lovers with real stakes · Book 1 out 2027 ↓ free prologue*
- `Writing about [topic] for [reader]. [Proof]. New essay every [day] ↓`
  > *Writing about money for first-gen professionals. Ex-banker. New essay every Sunday ↓*

**Link:** straight to the newsletter signup, or a link hub with the newsletter first.

**Lead magnet (the reason to subscribe):** free first chapter, a short story set in the same world, a one-page reading list, or an essay series. Ship one before launch.

## 4. Content pillars (pick 3–4)

| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **The work** | 30% | Short excerpts, one-line hooks, cover reveal, "a line I cut from chapter 4" |
| **The process** | 25% | Writing routine, word-count updates, how you research, rejection stories |
| **The taste** | 25% | What you're reading, recommendations, comp titles ("if you loved X…") |
| **The person** | 20% | Why you write this, your desk, your life around the work |

Fiction leans more on *the work* and *the taste*. Non-fiction leans more on *ideas*: turn each essay into 3–5 standalone posts.

## 5. Rhythm (sustainable for someone who also has to write)

- **Newsletter:** 1×/week, or every 2 weeks at minimum. Same day every time.
- **Social:** 3–4 posts/week. Each newsletter issue becomes 3 social posts.
- **Engagement:** 20 minutes/day commenting on writers and readers in your genre. Leave real comments, not "great post!"

## 6. Growth moves that actually work for writers

1. **Swap recommendations** with 2–3 newsletter writers of similar size (Substack Recommendations are built for this).
2. **Guest posts:** write one piece a month for a bigger newsletter or publication in your niche.
3. **Join reader communities** (Goodreads groups, genre Discords, subreddits) and contribute before you promote.
4. **Fiction:** short videos that open with the *trope* or *hook*, not "hi, I'm an author."
5. **Non-fiction:** one strong opinion per post. Hedged ideas don't travel.

## 7. Realistic milestones

| Day | Subscribers | Main platform followers | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 50–150 | 150–400 | Friends + early strangers |
| 60 | 150–400 | 400–1,000 | Strangers subscribing without being asked |
| 90 | 300–800 | 700–2,000 | People replying to emails = a real audience |

Below these at day 60? Usually the cause is a vague positioning line or a weak lead magnet. Fix those before posting more.

## 8. Mistakes to avoid
- Posting only "buy my book." Aim for 1 in 5 posts as a direct ask, no more.
- Waiting until the book is out. Start 12–18 months before publication if you can.
- Copying a big author's style on a brand-new account. They're coasting on an audience they already have.

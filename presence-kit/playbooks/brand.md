# Playbook: Brand

Product companies, shops, restaurants, studios, consumer services.

**The result that matters: visits that turn into sales or bookings.** Followers who never click are an audience you're paying to entertain.

---

## 1. Platform choice

| Brand type | Main | Second | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fashion / beauty / home / food products** | Instagram + TikTok | Pinterest | Visual discovery; Pinterest traffic keeps arriving for months |
| **Local business** (café, salon, gym, clinic) | Instagram + Google Business Profile | Facebook | Local search and the neighbourhood community |
| **B2B / services** | LinkedIn company page + founder's LinkedIn | YouTube | Buyers research before they talk to sales |
| **Digital product / app** | TikTok or X | YouTube Shorts | Demos and "how I use it" content |

Make one short video and post it to TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. Shoot vertical once, publish three times.

## 2. Positioning formula

> For **[specific customer]** who **[need or frustration]**, **[Brand]** is the **[category]** that **[one clear difference]**, unlike **[the usual alternative]**.

Then compress it into a **tagline of 7 words or fewer** for the bio.

## 3. Identity basics (needed before the first post)

| Asset | Minimum viable version |
|---|---|
| Logo | One mark that works small (avatar) and one wordmark |
| Colours | 1 main + 1 neutral + 1 accent, with hex codes written down |
| Fonts | 1 heading + 1 body font, used everywhere |
| Voice | 3 "we are / we are not" pairs (e.g. *warm, not cutesy*) |
| Photo style | One sentence: "natural light, real people, neutral backgrounds" |

Canva Brand Kit is enough at this stage. A full brand agency can wait until revenue justifies it.

## 4. Profile setup

**Handle:** brand name, the same everywhere (`@brand`, `@brandhq`, `@shopbrand`, `@brand.co`).
**Avatar:** the logomark, centred, readable at 40px.
**Bio formula:** `[What you sell] for [who] · [differentiator] · [proof or location] ↓ [offer]`
> *Small-batch hot sauce for people who want flavour, not just heat · Made in Lagos · 10% off first order ↓*

**Link:** shop or booking page, or a link hub with "Shop" first.
**Highlights / pinned posts:** Best-sellers · Reviews · How it's made · FAQ / Shipping.

## 5. Content pillars

| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Product in use** | 35% | Real customers using it, before/after, "3 ways to wear or use it" |
| **Behind the scenes** | 25% | How it's made, packing orders, the team, sourcing |
| **Proof** | 20% | Reviews, customer reposts, press, numbers ("1,000 orders shipped") |
| **Value / culture** | 20% | Tips, trends, and the lifestyle around the product, with no hard sell |

## 6. Rhythm

- **Feed / video:** 3–5×/week (at least 2 short videos).
- **Stories:** daily, low effort. Polls, packing orders, quick questions.
- **Engagement:** 30 min/day. Reply to every comment in the first hour; comment on 10 customer and partner accounts.

## 7. Growth moves that actually work for new brands

1. **Seed micro-creators:** send product to 20 creators with 2k–20k followers and strong engagement. Expect roughly 1 in 4 to post. That's normal.
2. **Reshare user content** with credit. It's free content and it's proof.
3. **Collaborate with neighbouring brands** (complementary, not competing) through giveaways or joint posts.
4. **Turn comments into content:** answer a question with a video reply.
5. **Only boost what already works:** once a post outperforms organically, put $5–10/day behind it. Never pay to push a post that didn't work for free.

## 8. Realistic milestones

| Day | Followers (main) | Monthly link clicks | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 200–500 | 50–150 | Profile looks alive; first DMs |
| 60 | 500–1,500 | 150–500 | Strangers tagging the brand |
| 90 | 1,000–3,000 | 400–1,200 | Sales you can trace to social |

Below these at day 60? Check in this order: **photo/video quality → offer and price → audience targeting.**

## 9. Mistakes to avoid
- Posting only polished product shots. They look like ads and get scrolled past like ads.
- Ignoring DMs. On a new account, every DM is a potential sale.
- Buying followers. It wrecks reach and credibility, and platforms detect it.

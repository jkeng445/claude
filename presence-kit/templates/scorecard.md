## Every review, answer these
1. **Top 3 posts by saves/shares.** What do they have in common? → Make more like them.
2. **Bottom 3 posts.** Kill that format or fix the hook?
3. **Did we post what we planned?** If not, the plan is too big. Cut it, don't feel guilty about it.
4. **One thing to try for the next 2 weeks:** ______

## Decision rules
- A format that performs best **3 reviews in a row** → make it the weekly signature.
- A pillar that performs worst **3 reviews in a row** → cut it or reshape it.
- Lots of reach but no link clicks → the bio or the offer is the problem, not the content.
- Followers growing but the result metric flat → wrong audience. Go back to the positioning worksheet.

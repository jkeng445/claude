# Intake questionnaire (Stage 1: Define)

Send this before the kickoff call, or run it live in 45 minutes. Don't start building until every **bold** question has an answer.

## About them
1. **Client type:** Writer / Brand / CEO / other: ______
2. **In one sentence, what do you do?**
3. What would you like to be *known for* in 12 months?
4. What do people already come to you for (advice, recommendations, skills)?
5. What do you believe about your field that many peers would disagree with?

## Audience
6. **Who exactly should follow you?** Describe one real person: role, age range, location, what they worry about.
7. Where does that person already spend time online?
8. Name 3–5 accounts your audience already follows and loves.

## Goal
9. **What should a follower *do*?** (subscribe / buy / book a call / hire / invite you to speak / other)
10. What does success look like at 90 days, and at 12 months?
11. Are there deadlines (book launch, product drop, fundraise, event)?

## Assets & constraints
12. Existing accounts, handles, website, email list (with numbers)?
13. Photos and video available? Are you comfortable on camera? (yes / with practice / no)
14. **Hours per week you can personally give:** ______
15. Budget for tools, photos, ads, creators: ______
16. Off-limits topics (clients, numbers, family, politics, competitors)?
17. Who approves posts, and how fast can they turn things around?

19. Three words you never want associated with you:
20. Accounts whose *tone* you admire (not necessarily same industry):

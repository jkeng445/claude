# Profile setup checklist (Stage 3: Build)

## Week 1: Claim & protect
- [ ] Pick the handle and check it on Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Threads, YouTube, Pinterest, and Substack
- [ ] Claim it on **all** of them, even unused ones (stops impersonators and keeps it for later)
- [ ] Domain (`name.com` / `brand.co`) bought, even if it only redirects for now
- [ ] Professional email on that domain
- [ ] **Two-factor authentication on every account** (use an authenticator app, not SMS, where offered)
- [ ] Logins stored in a password manager. **Clients keep ownership of their own accounts**; you get delegated access (Meta Business Suite, LinkedIn page admin, etc.) wherever the platform supports it
- [ ] Brands: business/professional account type switched on (unlocks analytics)

## Week 2: Dress the profiles
- [ ] Profile photo / logo (same image everywhere)
- [ ] Banner/cover where the platform has one
- [ ] Bio from the positioning worksheet, adapted to each platform's character limit
- [ ] One link → link hub or owned page, **tested on a phone**
- [ ] Highlights / pinned posts / Featured section set up
- [ ] Contact option (email button, booking link, or "DM me for…")
- [ ] Brands: Google Business Profile (if there's a physical location or local service area)

## Week 2: Bank content before launch
- [ ] **9 posts** published or scheduled (an Instagram grid of 3×3 looks complete)
  - 3 × introduction / who we are / why
  - 3 × pillar content
  - 3 × proof (work, product, testimonial, credential)
- [ ] 2 weeks of further posts drafted
- [ ] Scheduling tool connected (Buffer, Later, Metricool, or native schedulers)
- [ ] Lead magnet or offer live (free chapter, discount, guide, booking)

## Launch day
- [ ] Announce to existing network: personal accounts, email, WhatsApp/close friends
- [ ] Ask 10 people personally to follow *and* engage with the first posts
- [ ] Block 30 min to reply to every comment in the first 2 hours

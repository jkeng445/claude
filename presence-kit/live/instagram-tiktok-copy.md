## Shop page essentials

- Price in the buyer's currency (GBP/USD/CAD), with delivery cost shown before
  checkout
- Each product page has a **story card**: the technique, the motif, who made it
- Say plainly on every listing whether it is **"Adire-pattern print"** or
  **"hand-dyed Adire"**
- Size guide in both inches and cm, with the model's height and size stated
- A returns policy you can actually honour

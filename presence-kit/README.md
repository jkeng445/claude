# Presence Kit: building a social identity from nothing

A repeatable system for taking a **writer, a brand, or a business CEO** from zero online presence to a credible, findable, growing one in 90 days.

Use it two ways:
1. **For yourself.** Follow the playbook that fits.
2. **As a service.** Run clients through it and charge for it (see `SERVICE-OFFER.md`).

---

## The truth up front (set this expectation with every client)

- **90 days builds credibility, not fame.** Realistically: a complete, consistent profile; 300–2,000 real followers depending on platform and niche; and the first inbound opportunities (DMs, readers, leads, press asks).
- **Consistency beats talent.** Someone who posts 3×/week for 12 weeks beats someone who posts 20 brilliant things in week 1 and then disappears.
- **One main platform, done well.** New accounts spread across five platforms grow on none of them.
- **Followers are a vanity number.** What counts is the result: email subscribers for writers, sales and leads for brands, opportunities and pipeline for CEOs.

## The 5-stage method (same for every client type)

| Stage | Weeks | Output | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Define** | 1 | Who they are, who they're for, and what they want people to *do* | `templates/intake-questionnaire.md` |
| **2. Position** | 1 | One-line positioning, 3–4 content pillars, voice rules | `templates/positioning-worksheet.md` |
| **3. Build** | 2 | Handles claimed, profiles fully set up, link hub, first 9 posts banked before launch | `templates/profile-setup-checklist.md` |
| **4. Show up** | 3–12 | Posting rhythm, daily engagement block, a weekly "signature" piece | `templates/30-day-calendar.csv` + the client-type playbook |

## The non-negotiables (every client, every platform)

1. **Same name and handle everywhere.** Claim it even on platforms you won't use yet.
2. **A face, or a mark that never changes.** People follow people. Brands get a logo; writers and CEOs get a real, recent, well-lit photo.
3. **A bio that answers three questions:** who you are, who you help or what you make, and why someone should follow.
4. **One link** to a link hub or a page you own, never a dead end.
5. **9 posts in the bank before you announce anything,** so new visitors see a living profile.
6. **30 minutes a day of engagement.** Commenting on others matters as much as your own posting, and in the first 60 days it matters *more*.
7. **Own the audience.** Push followers to an email list. Platforms change their rules; an email list doesn't.
